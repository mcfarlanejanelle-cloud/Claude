Click to add a note. Drag to move it, drag its right edge to resize, right-click to delete, double-click to change the lyric. Green marks show the runs and grace notes she adds.

NOTE:BEATS/lyric · R:1 is a rest · _ continues the syllable · [HH AY] spells sounds

Syllables go onto the notes in order. Use _ to stretch a syllable over the next note, and hyphens to split words yourself (drift-ing).

Character

Breath, soft attacks, stacked doubles, long reverb.

Jazz-inflected runs, flips, scoops and a livelier vibrato.

Restraint: straight tone, late vibrato, laid-back timing, sighing falls.

Fine tuning

Lumen is a synthetic voice built from scratch (glottal source, formant vocal tract, phrasing rules). It does not imitate or clone any real singer. Everything runs in your browser.
